What it is

Not a website. Not a link page.

A website is built for reading. A link page is a list of places to go next. SKANI sits between them and is built for doing — the handful of things your customer actually opened their phone to do.

Website

Built for reading

Pages, sections, scrolling. Good when someone wants the full story. Slow when they just want the menu.

SKANI

Built for doing

Cards that open a menu, build an order, play a 360° tour, start a call, load the map, take a booking. One tap, and the thing happens.

Link page

Built for pointing

A list of links that sends people somewhere else. Nothing happens on the page itself.

Questions

The things people ask first.

What does it actually cost?

TZS 200,000 once to build it, then TZS 35,000 a month. The monthly is paid in blocks — six months minimum, which is TZS 210,000, or a full year for TZS 360,000 instead of 420,000.

So you start at TZS 410,000 on six months, or TZS 560,000 for the year. Nothing is taken from your sales.

Why a six-month minimum?

Because the monthly is not just hosting — it is us making your changes. A SKANI needs a few months in real use before it settles, and we would rather commit to that properly than hand you a link and disappear.

Can I change prices myself?

Not yet — today you message us and we make the change, usually the same day. That is included in the monthly and there is no limit on it.

We are building the self-service editor now, where you will change prices and items yourself. Existing customers move onto it first, at no extra cost.

Where do orders arrive?

On WhatsApp, as a complete message — every item, every option, already priced and totalled. The difference from today is that your customer builds it themselves in the SKANI, so nothing is guessed, mistyped or missed at the busy hour.

Do I need a website already?

No. Most of the businesses we work with have never had one. If you do have a website, a SKANI sits alongside it — it is the thing you put on a table tent, a receipt, a flyer, or your Instagram bio.

Do my customers need to download anything?

No. It opens in the phone's browser like any link. That is deliberate — nobody downloads an app to order a coffee.

I do not have a proper menu file. Is that a problem?

Not at all. A photo of a printed menu, a PDF, or even a list in a WhatsApp message is enough. We put it in and send it back for you to check before anything goes live.

What happens to my QR codes if something changes?

Nothing. Your codes point at a fixed address, and what sits behind it is what changes. You will never reprint a table tent because you added a dish.

How long until it is live?

Usually two to four days from the first conversation, depending on how big the menu, room list or catalogue is. Rush jobs are possible — ask us.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your SKANI comes down and the link stops working, so anything you have printed would stop leading anywhere. Tell us before the term ends and we will sort out something sensible.
