Get the Best Results

1

Place where customers pause

Tables, counter, entrance, and waiting areas are the strongest scan points.

2

Make it easy to see and scan

Avoid dark corners, glass glare, and very small prints — if it's hard to scan, people won't try twice.

3

Tell people what to expect

Simple copy like "Scan to explore Cane Crush, Anjari & more" gets far more scans.

4

Test before printing in bulk

Scan with both Android and iPhone first, so you don't pay for a print run that doesn't work.

5

Use your link online too

Add it to WhatsApp replies, Instagram bio, and Google Business for traffic beyond physical scans.

6

Keep your Skani updated

Refresh menus, prices, and offers regularly — fresh content keeps customers coming back.
